Our Mission

The CTO works to monitor and protect bird populations across Centrala municipality. By tracking sightings and behaviors, we help inform environmental planning to create a more bird-friendly urban landscape.

Why Birds Matter

  • Birds are indicators of environmental health
  • Birdwatching improves mental wellbeing
  • Urban birds help control pests
  • Birds pollinate plants and disperse seeds
  • They contribute to biodiversity
  • Birds inspire art and culture
  • They connect people with nature
  • Birds contribute to scientific research
